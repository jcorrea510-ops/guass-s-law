Physics reference
Coulomb's law
Superposition of point-charge fields
Potential (V = 0 at infinity)
Field ↔ potential
  • Field lines start on + charges and end on − charges (or at infinity); the number of lines is proportional to the charge.
  • Line density ∝ field strength. Lines never cross.
  • Equipotentials are everywhere ⊥ to field lines; E points toward decreasing V.
  • Moving along an equipotential takes zero work.
  • A + test charge accelerates toward lower V; a − charge toward higher V. Total energy K + U is conserved.
