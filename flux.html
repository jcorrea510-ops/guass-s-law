What to notice
  • Change the shape or size of the surface: the total flux doesn't change as long as the same charges are enclosed.
  • A charge outside contributes zero net flux: the field lines it sends in come back out (blue patches balance orange ones).
  • But outside charges do change E on the surface — the colouring changes even though Φ does not.
  • Orange dots mark field lines leaving the surface, blue dots lines entering. Lines from an outside charge that enter always leave again.
  • Move a charge across the surface and watch Φ jump by q/ε₀.
